# Stakeholders

## Stakeholder List

| Stakeholder | Role in Process | Interest / Concern |
|---|---|---|
| Employee (Claimant) | Submits expense claims | Fast, transparent reimbursement; minimal paperwork |
| Direct Manager | Approves claims | Quick, low-effort review; confidence claims are legitimate |
| Finance/Audit Team | Verifies and processes payment | Accurate, well-documented, policy-compliant claims |
| Finance Head | Oversees budget/policy | Cost control, audit compliance, process efficiency |
| HR/Admin (indirect) | Manages expense policy | Consistent policy application across departments |

## Power / Interest Grid

| Stakeholder | Power | Interest | Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Finance Head | High | High | Manage Closely |
| Direct Manager | Medium | High | Keep Involved |
| Finance/Audit Team | Medium | High | Keep Involved |
| Employee | Low | High | Keep Informed |
| HR/Admin | Medium | Low | Keep Satisfied |
