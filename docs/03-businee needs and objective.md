# Business Need & Objectives

## Business Need

ABC (Pvt) Ltd needs a better-defined, more transparent expense claim process in order to:

- Reduce reimbursement turnaround time
- Give employees visibility into claim status
- Reduce receipt loss and documentation errors
- Reduce manual verification effort for Finance/Audit
- Ensure claims comply with company expense policy

## Business Objectives

This project aims to:

1. Analyze the current (AS-IS) expense claim process end-to-end
2. Identify root causes of delays and errors
3. Define business and functional requirements for an improved process
4. Design a TO-BE process that addresses the identified pain points
5. Propose a solution (process changes and/or lightweight tooling) and a plan to deliver it
