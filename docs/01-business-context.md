# Business Context

ABC (Pvt) Ltd is a hypothetical mid-size company with approximately 150–200 employees across Sales, Operations, Finance, and Administration departments.

## Common Expense Claim Types

Employees regularly incur work-related expenses, including:

- **Travel** — client visit transport, fuel, taxi/ride-hailing fares
- **Accommodation** — hotel stays for out-of-town assignments
- **Meals & entertainment** — client meetings, team meals during travel
- **Office supplies** — stationery and small equipment purchased by staff
- **Communication** — mobile/data costs for work purposes
- **Training** — external courses or certification fees
- **Miscellaneous** — printing, courier, and other small reimbursable costs

## Current Reimbursement Process (High Level)

1. Employee incurs an expense and retains the receipt.
2. Employee fills a claim form (paper or Excel) and attaches receipts.
3. Claim is submitted to the employee's direct manager for approval.
4. Manager manually reviews and signs off.
5. Approved claims are forwarded to the Finance/Audit team for verification.
6. Finance processes the reimbursement and pays the employee.

There is no centralized system. Claims are tracked informally via email, paper, or spreadsheets, and there is no shared visibility into a claim's current status.
