# Project Scope

## In Scope

- Analysis of the current (AS-IS) expense claim and reimbursement process
- Identification of pain points and root causes through gap analysis
- Definition of business and functional requirements for an improved process
- Design of a TO-BE process
- A high-level proposed solution (process redesign and/or lightweight tooling recommendation)
- Agile project plan (backlog, sprint plan) for how the solution would be delivered
- Risk assessment and stakeholder management plan
- Sample data analysis illustrating claim turnaround time and delay patterns

## Out of Scope

- Actual development or deployment of a software system
- Integration with real company payroll or accounting systems
- Legal or tax compliance review of expense policy
- Real employee or financial data (all data used is fictional/simulated)

Defining scope boundaries explicitly is a core Business Analysis practice — it prevents scope creep and sets clear expectations for what the project will and will not deliver.
