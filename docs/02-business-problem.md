# Business Problem

ABC (Pvt) Ltd has experienced recurring issues in its expense claim and reimbursement process, including:

- Missing or lost receipts, causing disputes or unreimbursed expenses
- Slow manager approval turnaround, delaying reimbursement by weeks in some cases
- No visibility for employees into where their claim currently stands
- Manual handling errors — miscalculations, duplicate claims, or misplaced paperwork
- Extra rework for the Finance/Audit team, who must manually verify incomplete or inconsistent claims

These issues cause employee frustration, delayed reimbursements, and increased administrative burden on Finance and Audit staff.

> **Note:** These are initial business hypotheses based on observed patterns. They are investigated and validated through the process analysis in this case study rather than treated as confirmed facts.
